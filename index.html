🧪 Slime Care Guide

Keep Your Slime Perfect!

01

Store in the container

Always keep your slime sealed in its original container.

💡 Keep the lid on when not playing!
02

Wash hands first

Play with clean, dry hands — no lotion!

💡 No lotion before slime time!
03

Keep it cool

Store away from sunlight. Heat makes slime sticky.

💡 A cool drawer works great!
04

Use activator if sticky

Add a tiny drop of activator and knead slowly.

💡 Less is more!
05

Use deactivator if stiff

Add a drop of lotion or glycerine if too hard.

💡 Warm in hands first!
06

Play regularly!

Regular play keeps slime in great condition.

💡 10 mins daily keeps slime happy!
